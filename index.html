How version 1 decides

Weighted votes come from EMA 9/21 trend, MACD 12/26/9, RSI 14, 60-minute VWAP, Bollinger position, 15-minute price momentum, and recent volume confirmation. A score of +3 or more = UP, -3 or less = DOWN, otherwise NEUTRAL. This is a heuristic signal, not a guaranteed forecast or trading instruction.

The page refreshes market data every 30 seconds while it is open. Mobile browsers may suspend JavaScript when the tab is backgrounded or the phone is locked, so do not rely on this page alone for unattended alerts.
